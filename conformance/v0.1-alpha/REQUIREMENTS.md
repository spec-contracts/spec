# SPEC v0.1-alpha normative requirements traceability

Status: exercise; not a certification matrix or certification decision.

This catalog accounts for all 43 uppercase `MUST`/`MUST NOT` occurrences in the pinned specification. Two occurrences define RFC keywords rather than protocol behavior, leaving 41 normative requirements. A list introduced by one keyword is kept as one requirement and its complete list is part of the verification scope.

Disposition totals:

- Tested: 18
- Partially tested: 6
- Inspection required: 9
- Deferred coverage gap: 8

`Tested` means the present draft suite or its verifier has executable evidence. `Partially tested` means at least one normative subcondition remains uncovered. `Inspection required` defines a human review obligation. `Deferred` is an explicit suite gap and cannot support certification yet.
