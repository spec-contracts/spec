# Governance notes for v0.1-alpha

SPEC is currently an alpha design stewarded through the `spec-contracts/spec`
repository. The present MIT license remains in force; this milestone does not
silently change licensing terms.

Before accepting substantive normative contributions, maintainers intend to
evaluate a standards-appropriate, Community Specification-style licensing and
governance model for normative prose and schemas. Runtime, validator, and other
implementation code should continue under an ordinary open-source software
license. Any change will be proposed and documented explicitly.

The intended direction is neutral multi-party stewardship after executable
evidence and external technical review. No foundation machinery is established
by this note, and nothing here implies Linux Foundation, Joint Development
Foundation, PDP-Connect, or any other third-party affiliation or endorsement.
